How this works

Nothing is stored here

This page keeps everything in memory only — close the tab and it is gone. That is deliberate: nothing about your body or your eating is saved into a file you carry around.

So the routine is: log during the day, then tap Copy day or Save check-in and paste it into the Fitness chat. Claude keeps the running history in the project logbook and rebuilds this page with your data already loaded into the charts.

Logging meals from photos

  • Snap the plate before you eat. One photo, two seconds.
  • Send it into the Fitness chat, with context if it helps (“a whole tin of chickpeas”, “half went to my son”).
  • You get calories and protein back; add them here with Something else.

Accuracy: comfortably ±15–20%. Anyone claiming better — app or human — is guessing with more confidence. That is fine: you are steering a weekly average, not dosing medication.

The portions behind the buttons

Each button is one standard portion as defined in your food playbook — 250 g skyr, 3 eggs, 150 g tofu, a 240 g tin of chickpeas. If you ate half, tap once and use the minus button; if you ate double, tap twice. Precision beyond that is not worth your attention.

Two weeks, then stop. Log properly for a fortnight so you learn what 40 g of protein and 550 kcal actually look like on a plate. After that, log only the meals you are unsure about and the weeks where progress has stalled. Anything that demands daily perfection will be the first thing to break when someone gets a cold.
Calorie and protein values are rounded typical figures for the portions listed. General nutrition guidance, not medical or dietary treatment.
